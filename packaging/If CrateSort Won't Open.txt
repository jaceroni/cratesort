CrateSort isn't from the Mac App Store, so the first time you open it,
macOS will warn you it can't verify the developer. That's expected —
here's how to get past it:

1. Try to open CrateSort. You'll see a message saying it can't verify
   the developer.

2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.

3. Scroll down. You'll see a line that says something like
   "CrateSort was blocked..." with an Open Anyway button next to it.

4. Click Open Anyway, then confirm one more time when it asks.

You only need to do this once — after that, CrateSort opens normally.
